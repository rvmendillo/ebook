Mistakes you repeat at full speed become habits. Slow, perfect repetitions build accuracy that survives when you speed up.

<!-- more -->

## The routine

1. Find your **target tempo** — say 120 BPM.
2. Set the metronome to **60%** of it: $120 \times 0.6 = 72$ BPM.
3. Play the passage **three times in a row with no mistakes**.
4. Raise the tempo by **4–5 BPM** and repeat.
5. If you make a mistake twice, drop back one step.

> [!TIP]
> Stop for the day when you reach a tempo you can't play cleanly three times. Your brain consolidates the skill overnight — tomorrow's starting point will be higher.

It feels slow. It *is* slow. And it is the fastest way to play fast.
