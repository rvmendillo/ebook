To multiply a two-digit number by 11, **split its digits apart and put their sum in the middle**.

$$
36 \times 11 \;\to\; 3\,\underline{(3+6)}\,6 = 396
$$

<!-- more -->

## When the sum is 10 or more

Carry the 1 into the first digit:

$$
78 \times 11 \;\to\; 7\,\underline{(7+8)}\,8 = 7\,\underline{15}\,8 \;\to\; 858
$$

## Why it works

$11 \times n = 10n + n$. Writing $n = 10a + b$:

$$
11(10a + b) = 100a + 10(a + b) + b
$$

The hundreds digit is $a$, the tens digit is $a + b$ and the ones digit is $b$ — exactly the trick.

> [!TIP]
> Try these: 45 × 11, 62 × 11, 99 × 11. (Answers: 495, 682, 1089.)
