Delete your Polymath account

Fastest: in the app

Open Polymath → Account → Delete account and confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email us from the address you used in Polymath (or include the Reader ID shown at the bottom of the Account screen). We delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Request deletion by email

What is deleted

Your account (email or guest ID), credit balance and history, items you own, and your subscription record.

Purchase records may be kept where the law requires (for example for tax). Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription — cancel it in your store account settings.
